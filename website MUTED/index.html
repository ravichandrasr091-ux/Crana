Technology

Built Different, at Every Layer

01

Electrostatic Precipitation

High-voltage ionization charges incoming particles. Aluminum collecting plates — spaced with micron-level precision — attract and trap them, eliminating the need for filter replacements.

02

Gravity Settling Chamber

Larger particles settle before entering the ESP stage, extending electrode life and maintaining peak efficiency across all operating modes.

03

Self-Cleaning Cycle

Automated acoustic pulse dislodges collected particles from electrodes. No tools required. No maintenance window. Runs quietly, stays clean.

04

Low Pressure Drop Design

Engineered airflow paths keep resistance under 900 Pa across all modes. Your HVAC system breathes freely. So do you.

05

Advanced Filtration System

Multi-layer HEPA filters capture ultrafine particles down to 0.3 microns, including allergens, bacteria, and viruses for comprehensive air purification.

06

Smart Monitoring and Control

Integrated sensors track air quality in real-time with app connectivity for remote monitoring, automated adjustments, and performance analytics.
